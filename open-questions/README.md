# Questions about rain

A non-exhaustive, unordered invitation to explore. All entries are open within Pluvia; this does not imply that they are unresolved in the literature. Add questions from any discipline, and document existing answers before proposing novel research.

| ID | Question | Areas | Possible approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| P01 | Why are falling raindrops not shaped like tears? | natural-sciences; mathematics-and-computation | Physics of droplets and imaging. |
| P02 | What produces the smell after rain, and how do humans perceive it? | natural-sciences; social-sciences | Chemistry, aerosol formation and sensory perception. |
| P03 | How do cloud particles become rain? | natural-sciences | Microphysics, nucleation, collision and coalescence. |
| P04 | How does precipitation differ across planets and moons? | natural-sciences | Atmospheric composition, phase transitions and evidence. |
| P05 | What strategies have plants evolved under contrasting rainfall regimes? | natural-sciences | Ecology, evolution and physiological trade-offs. |
| P06 | How can rain fields be modeled as stochastic spatial processes? | mathematics-and-computation | Spatial dependence, intermittency and extremes. |
| P07 | What limits the predictability of local rain? | mathematics-and-computation; natural-sciences | Dynamics, initial uncertainty, observations and scale. |
| P08 | How can synthetic rainfall preserve dry spells and extreme events? | mathematics-and-computation | Stochastic generators and validation. |
| P09 | How can one compare rain fields that are displaced in space? | mathematics-and-computation | Spatial verification, transport distances and scale. |
| P10 | How have different communities understood and responded to the arrival of seasonal rain? | humanities | Context-specific ethnography and local knowledge. |
| P11 | What roles does rain play in particular mythologies and religious traditions? | humanities | Comparative interpretation without flattening cultural differences. |
| P12 | How can historical rainfall and drought be reconstructed from archives and proxies? | humanities; natural-sciences | Source criticism and paleoclimate uncertainty. |
| P13 | How do languages distinguish kinds of rain? | humanities | Lexical semantics and attested usage. |
| P14 | How have rainfall variability and water institutions interacted in historical societies? | humanities; social-sciences | Multiple causes; avoid climatic determinism. |
| P15 | When and why do people find rain sounds soothing? | social-sciences | Psychology, acoustics and individual variation. |
| P16 | How does rain affect everyday mobility and work? | social-sciences | Urban observation, time use and inequality. |
| P17 | Who bears the costs of rainfall extremes in cities? | social-sciences; technology | Exposure, infrastructure and environmental justice. |
| P18 | How do rainfall risks influence agricultural decisions and insurance? | social-sciences; technology | Economics, risk and institutions. |
| P19 | How does the meaning of rain vary across films, poems and paintings? | arts-and-culture; humanities | Close reading, historical context and medium. |
| P20 | How have composers and sound artists represented rain? | arts-and-culture | Musical structure, recording and interpretation. |
| P21 | How has architecture adapted to rain across climates and cultures? | arts-and-culture; technology; humanities | Material culture, construction and climate. |
| P22 | How do gauges, radar and satellites measure or infer precipitation? | technology; natural-sciences | Measurement principles and uncertainty. |
| P23 | What can rainwater harvesting achieve under different seasonal regimes? | technology | Water balance, engineering and practical constraints. |
| P24 | How can probabilistic rain warnings be communicated effectively? | technology; social-sciences | Forecast verification, communication and user needs. |

No priority or fixed order. Choose by curiosity, feasibility and available evidence.
