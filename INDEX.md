# Knowledge index

This is a living map, not a syllabus. All current areas are exploratory; no completed articles are implied.

## [Natural sciences](natural-sciences/README.md)
- Physics and chemistry of rain, droplets, aerosols and petrichor.
- Meteorology, cloud microphysics, climatology and hydrology.
- Biology, ecology, evolution and planetary precipitation.

## [Mathematics and computation](mathematics-and-computation/README.md)
- Geometry and dynamics of falling drops.
- Stochastic processes, spatial statistics, extreme values and uncertainty.
- Numerical modeling, forecasting, inverse problems and visualization.

## [Humanities](humanities/README.md)
- Anthropology, ethnography, Indigenous and local weather knowledge.
- History, archaeology, religion, mythology and philosophy.
- Linguistics, naming, metaphors and meanings of rain.

## [Social sciences](social-sciences/README.md)
- Psychology, perception, sound, memory and emotion.
- Sociology, economics, politics, migration and environmental justice.
- Institutions, collective responses and water governance.

## [Arts and culture](arts-and-culture/README.md)
- Rain in literature, poetry, painting, music, cinema and photography.
- Architecture, aesthetics, material culture and everyday life.

## [Technology](technology/README.md)
- Rain gauges, radar, satellites and other observation systems.
- Engineering, drainage, water harvesting, agriculture and adaptation.
- Forecast communication and early warning systems.
